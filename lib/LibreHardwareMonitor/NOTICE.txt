Falco bundles LibreHardwareMonitorLib 0.9.6.

Project: https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor
License: Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0)

The bundled library is used as an optional sensor provider. If it cannot be
loaded or the firmware exposes no value, Falco falls back to the Windows ACPI
temperature provider.
